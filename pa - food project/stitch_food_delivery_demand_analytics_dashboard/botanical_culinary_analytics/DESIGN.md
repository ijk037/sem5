## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an academic culinary intelligence environment grounded in calm, organic composure. Departing completely from aggressive neon telemetry, hyperactive dark-mode dashboards, and visual technocracy, it interprets demand forecasting through the lens of a quiet, sunlit kitchen atelier and refined botanical editorial press.

The visual ethos blends **Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, unhurried negative space that treats forecasting models, historical charts, and ingredient inventory sheets as works of deliberate scholarship.
- Natural textures, organic tints, and soft cream foundations resembling tactile oatmeal paper and linen bindery.
- Evocative culinary accents through flowing script flourishes that soften analytical density, bringing an artisan human sensibility to predictive computation.
- An emotional resonance of unhurried precision, quiet confidence, and sensory clarity.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from garden flora, raw ceramic slip, and culinary hearthcraft, prioritizing soft perceptual luminosity and restful contrast.

- **Background Canvas**: Soft Cream / Warm Oatmeal Paper (`#FBFBF8` primary canvas, shifting to `#F5F6F1` for recessed structural segments and sub-canvases).
- **Surface**: Warm Ivory White (`#FFFFFF`) framed strictly by faint, whisper-thin borders (`#E3E6DC`).
- **Primary (Sage Reserve)**: Soft Sage Green (`#768F7B` core, `#849B89` hover/tint), establishing restorative structural elements, key status indicators, and confident yet soothing primary signals.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Ember)**: Muted Earthy Terracotta (`#C27A5B`), deployed intentionally for active tab indicators, high-conviction pill CTA buttons, threshold anomalies, and predictive peak flags.
- **Typography & Neutral**: Deep Forest Charcoal (`#2C352E`) provides crisp, highly legible editorial contrast without the stark harshness of absolute black. Muted text steps down to `#5A665D` for auxiliary notes and `#8A968C` for subtle metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes academic research rigor with the timeless warmth of culinary recipe folios:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs classical transitional serifs to command page rhythm with grace. Display variants use light-to-medium cuts that preserve airiness.
- **Analytical & Body Infrastructure (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear, contemporary legibility for complex tabular forecasts, confidence bands, parameters, and time horizons.
- **Italic Script Accents (`Playfair Display Italic`)**: Applied to analytical footnotes, culinary provenance subtitles, observational side-notes, and badge flourishes to punctuate data tables with handcrafted distinction.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid, breathable layout with balanced horizontal boundaries prevents cognitive strain across complex multivariate forecast models.

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max content container of 1360px centered on screen. Generous outer margins of `3rem` and gutters of `1.5rem` ensure analytical cards never crowd.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters held at `1.25rem`, with margins scaled to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Single-column stack with outer margins of `1.25rem` and internal component padding reduced to `space-md` (`1rem`).
- **Whitespace Cadence**: Section dividers rely on `space-xl` (`2.75rem`) vertical voids rather than heavy divider rules. Data density is balanced by preserving internal card padding of at least `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), giving charts and tables room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through tactile paper layering and translucent tonality, rejecting synthetic drop shadows and glossy neon refractions.

- **Base Elevation (Canvas)**: Background canvas sits at `#FBFBF8` with subtle section recesses tinted in `#F5F6F1`.
- **Container Elevation (Sheets)**: Surface cards emerge as crisp Ivory White (`#FFFFFF`) plates outlined with delicate, hairline borders of `1px solid #E3E6DC`.
- **Layered Popovers & Flyouts**: Floating inspector panels, date-range selectors, and model tooltips utilize an ethereal, warm ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(44, 53, 46, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 53, 46, 0.03);`
- **Focus & Selection**: Outlines take a soft botanical ring of `2px solid rgba(118, 143, 123, 0.4)` offset by 2px against pure ivory backgrounds.

## Shapes

The geometric structure balances architectural stability with soft ergonomic comfort:

- **Cards & Data Panels**: Structured at `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for a composed, paper-like presence.
- **Pill Elements & Action Triggers**: High-emphasis interactive elements, such as active time toggles, primary CTA buttons, and status indicator chips, adopt full pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize organic fluidity.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Subtle 8px (`rounded-md`) curvature maintaining clear definition inside dense statistical configuration panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill (Terracotta Active)**: Background `#C27A5B`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Padding `0.625rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover state gently shifts to `#B16B4D`.
- **Secondary Pill (Sage Subtle)**: Background `#768F7B`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#69806E`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent surface with `#E3E6DC` border, text `#2C352E`, transitioning to `#F5F6F1` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Delicate pill-shaped tags (`border: 1px solid #E3E6DC; background: #FFFFFF;`).
- **Peak Demand Indicator**: Tinted `#C27A5B` at 10% opacity, text `#C27A5B`, paired with an italic editorial script label (`Playfair Display Italic`).
- **Baseline Normal**: Tinted `#768F7B` at 12% opacity, text `#2C352E`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E6DC`, text `#2C352E`, placeholder `#8A968C`. Border subtly shifts to `#768F7B` on focus with no dramatic glow.
- **Time Window Segmented Controls**: Oatmeal container (`#F5F6F1`) with pill slider switches; active state highlights in solid `#FFFFFF` with whisper shadow and charcoal text.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- **Forecast Sheets**: Solid `#FFFFFF` backgrounds bound by `1px solid #E3E6DC`. Headers utilize `Playfair Display` titles matched with italicized demand commentary below.
- **Stat Summary Tiles**: Flat ivory containers with oversized numerical readouts in deep charcoal, paired with muted terracotta upward drift indicators and soft sage downward trend markers.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes**: 18px rounded square (4px radius) bordered with `#E3E6DC`. When selected, fills with `#768F7B` displaying a clean white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Pill-concentric ring styling; active state displays a `#C27A5B` core dot encircled by warm cream spacing.

### Tables & Lists
- **Tabular Forecast Lists**: Striped backgrounds avoid harsh contrasts; alternating rows shift between `#FFFFFF` and `#FAFBF8`. Dividers use ultra-fine 1px `#E3E6DC` lines with cell padding set generously at `1rem 1.25rem`.
